BIOAbout Elijah

Elijah Stone is a New York–based singer-songwriter whose originals blend pop, rock, and soul into a fresh yet timeless sound. His debut EP Twenty-Second Catch was released in 2025, showcasing groove-forward, hook-driven songs with candid lyricism. Following that, he released his first music video for “Look Listen” and completed a 22-show summer tour of the northeast. He has a new album forthcoming, and is currently booking shows for 2027. Elijah’s live show is fun, dynamic, and good for all ages.

SHOW & TECHSet lengths, gear, setup

The show

  • Solo original pop show with a full-band feel: he sings over tracks he produced himself, builds songs live with a loop pedal, and includes stripped-back guitar and vocal songs
  • 20+ originals, with crowd-favorite covers for longer sets
  • All ages, indoors or outdoors, day or night
  • Set lengths from 45 minutes to 3 hours

Setup

  • Fully self-contained; all gear fits on one cart
  • Brings his own PA (Bose S1 Pro+), best for small and medium spaces
  • Can plug into a house PA for bigger stages
  • Fits in a 6' × 8' space

Gear

  • Electric guitar, pedalboard, and amp
  • Loop pedal and drum machine
  • Vocal mic and stand
  • Bose S1 Pro+ PA

Travel

  • Based in New York City
  • Drives to shows across the Northeast with his own gear

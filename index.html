About

Elijah Stone is a New York–based singer-songwriter whose originals blend pop, rock, and soul into a fresh yet timeless sound. His debut EP Twenty-Second Catch was released in 2025, showcasing groove-forward, hook-driven songs with candid lyricism. Following that, he released his first music video for “Look Listen” and completed a 22-show summer tour of the Northeast. He has a new album forthcoming, and is currently booking shows for 2027.
